# Verdict

## NO_VERDICT

This repository contains contracts, render-only tooling, schemas, and
placeholders. It contains no live SGLang run, no benchmark measurements, no
canary response, and no claim of throughput, latency, quality, or successful
model execution.

The only admissible public evidence status in this foundation is one of
`NO_VERDICT`, `NOT_RUN`, `DIAGNOSTIC`, or `DISQUALIFIED`. A future exact-SHA
gate may produce `PHASE0_PASS` for launch admission, but that is not a model
or serving verdict.
